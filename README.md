An original open-world crime game set in **Solmar Bay**, a sun-soaked coastal city of our own — no Rockstar names, maps, characters or art anywhere in it.

## The game

You are a new wheelman in Rosa's crew. Explore the city **on foot and by car**, steal anything with wheels, outrun the Solmar Bay Police under a **5-star wanted system**, and run **six story missions** — from your first stolen car to the bank job and the last score: the mayor's Falco GT, delivered to the yacht *Sirena*.

- **A living city**: downtown towers, the marina with moored boats, Playa Dorada beach, the Las Palmas suburbs and the industrial north — with traffic that follows the roads, pedestrians who flee gunfire, and palm-lined streets.
- **Crime and heat**: stealing cars, shooting and wrecking cars raise your wanted level; patrol cars chase and ram you, officers try to bust you on foot, and the heat only fades when you lay low — or reach the safehouse.
- **Missions and story**: six jobs with dialogue from Rosa, Luz and El Toro, timed deliveries, escapes and a bank heist, paying cash and respect.
- **Side work**: taxi fares and cash stashes pay on the side; respect opens the city.
- **Feel and polish**: arcade car handling with skids, a HUD with wanted stars, health, cash and speed, a **live minimap** with mission, police and player blips, synthesized WebAudio sound (engine, sirens, gunshots, a sunset bass line), title / pause / busted / wasted screens, and a local save.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Drive or walk (Shift to run on foot) |
| E | Enter / steal a car, exit a car, pick up a fare |
| F or click | Shoot (on foot) |
| M | Start the next mission |
| P / Esc | Pause |
| Enter | Start / restart |

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server (`node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080) — the game runs fully offline, no network requests, no build step.
